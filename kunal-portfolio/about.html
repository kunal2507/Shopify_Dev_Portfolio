My story

I lead Shopify development at Boult Audio, one of India's fastest-growing consumer electronics brands, where I work on theme architecture, conversion optimization and frontend performance for a high-volume D2C storefront.

Before that, I built production sites for D2C labels, furniture companies, healthcare clinics, restaurants, agencies and global e-commerce stores — from fully custom Liquid themes to headless Next.js storefronts on the Storefront API. Thirty-four of those builds are live in production today.

What ties them together is how the work is measured: in conversion lifts, faster load times and revenue — not shipped pages.

How I solve problems

Every engagement starts with the numbers that matter — not the homepage. Discovery defines what "working" means for the business; strategy sets the architecture and conversion plan; design shapes flows around real content; development enforces performance budgets from the first commit; and optimization keeps measuring after launch.

Two rules never bend: real content over lorem ipsum, and accessibility never sacrificed for visual effects. The 95+ average Lighthouse score across launches exists because speed is treated as a constraint, not a polish pass.
