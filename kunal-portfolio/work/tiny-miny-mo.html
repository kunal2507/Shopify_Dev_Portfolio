Tiny Miny Mo — a toy store parents can navigate.

A premium Shopify experience for a toy retailer with a large catalog — built to stay intuitive for parents.

CategoryPremium toy retail
RoleDesign & development
StackShopify Plus · Liquid · Storefront API
Livetinyminymo.com ↗

Overview

Tiny Miny Mo is a children's boutique with a catalog large enough to overwhelm — hundreds of products across ages, categories and price points. The brief: a premium shopping experience where a busy parent finds the right toy in seconds, not scrolls.

The problem

Develop a premium Shopify experience for a toy retailer managing a large catalog, while keeping navigation intuitive for parents and ensuring excellent shopping performance. Big catalogs usually mean slow pages and deep menu trees — both are conversion killers.

The build

A completely customized Shopify theme, structured around how parents actually shop:

  • Optimized collection architecture with age-bracket browsing
  • Engaging product pages with quick views
  • Custom sections for merchandising without code changes
  • Bundle upsells woven through the funnel
  • Seamless browsing across 250+ live products

Technical notes

The catalog is the constraint everything is designed around. Collection architecture does the heavy lifting so no page ever has to render everything; quick views cut a whole page-load out of product discovery; and the upsell logic lives in the funnel where intent is highest rather than as a bolt-on widget.

Impact

A premium toy shopping experience supporting 250+ live products with fast navigation, conversion-focused design — and +31% upsell conversion.
