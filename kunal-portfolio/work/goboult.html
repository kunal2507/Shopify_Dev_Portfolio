GoBoult — commerce at enterprise scale.

Leading Shopify engineering for one of India's fastest-growing consumer electronics brands.

CategoryEnterprise Shopify
RoleLead Shopify Developer
StackShopify Plus · Liquid · GraphQL
Livegoboult.co.in ↗

Overview

Boult Audio is a high-volume D2C electronics brand, and its Shopify storefront is where the growth actually happens. As Lead Shopify Developer, I own the storefront's theme architecture, conversion optimization and frontend performance — the layer between the brand's marketing engine and the checkout button.

The problem

Scale one of India's fastest-growing consumer electronics brands while supporting high traffic, enterprise integrations, and continuous feature development — without sacrificing performance. New campaigns, new markets and new commerce formats land constantly, and every one of them has to ship fast and stay fast.

The build

I led Shopify development across the storefront, building the systems that let the brand move quickly:

  • Custom theme sections designed for reuse across campaigns
  • Influencer commerce experiences and shoppable video
  • Shopify Markets for multi-region selling
  • GTM implementation and CRM integrations
  • Metafield architecture that keeps content structured and editable
  • Checkout optimizations, including GoKwik integration

Technical notes

At this traffic level, small decisions compound. Theme sections are built to be composed rather than duplicated, metafields carry structured product data so merchandising doesn't require code changes, and the analytics layer (GTM, CRM) is wired so growth decisions run on trustworthy data. Performance budgets hold even as features ship weekly.

Impact

Enterprise Shopify engineering powering millions of customer interactions across India's leading D2C audio brand.
