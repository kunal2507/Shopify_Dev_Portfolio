Callas — from marketplace to brand home.

A premium direct-to-consumer storefront for a furniture brand previously selling primarily through Amazon.

CategoryFurniture commerce
RoleDesign & development
StackShopify Plus · Three.js · Liquid
Livecallas.in ↗

Overview

Callas sold furniture, but Amazon owned the relationship. The project was about building the brand a home of its own — a storefront premium enough to justify buying direct, and robust enough to carry an extensive product catalog.

The problem

Create a premium direct-to-consumer storefront for a furniture brand previously selling primarily through Amazon, while showcasing an extensive product catalog. On a marketplace, every listing looks the same; on your own storefront, the experience is the differentiation.

The build

A fully customized Shopify theme built to feel like the brand, not the platform:

  • Premium UI components designed for furniture merchandising
  • Custom product layouts that let large pieces breathe
  • Optimized shopping journeys across more than 100 products
  • Three.js and a custom API powering richer product presentation

Technical notes

Furniture is a considered purchase — buyers linger, compare and come back. Product layouts are built for that rhythm: generous imagery, structured specification data, and journeys tuned so a returning visitor lands back where their decision left off.

Impact

A premium standalone brand presence established beyond the marketplace, supporting a catalog of 100+ furniture products.
