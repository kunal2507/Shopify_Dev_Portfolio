Blue Ridge — B2B commerce for the US market.

A B2B-focused platform for a North Carolina cabinet supplier serving contractors and homeowners.

CategoryUS B2B commerce
RoleDesign & development
StackShopify Plus · JavaScript · Custom Liquid
LiveClient project · US

Overview

Blue Ridge Cabinet Connection supplies cabinets and related services across North Carolina. Unlike a consumer store, its customers are contractors and homeowners mid-project — people who need a quote, not a cart. The platform had to sell services and generate qualified leads.

The problem

Develop a B2B-focused platform for a cabinet supplier offering multiple services, while generating qualified contractor and homeowner leads. In the US remodeling and construction market, trust signals and a frictionless path to a quote decide who gets the job.

The build

Custom Shopify work shaped around lead generation rather than checkout:

  • Custom Shopify pages built for each service line
  • Service-focused layouts that qualify visitors as they read
  • Quote generation flows that turn interest into a conversation
  • Conversion-optimized sections tailored to the US remodeling market

Technical notes

B2B commerce inverts the usual Shopify assumptions: the conversion isn't a purchase, it's a qualified inquiry. Page structure, form placement and section flow are all tuned to move a contractor from "browsing options" to "requesting a quote" with as little friction as possible.

Impact

Premium Shopify delivery for international B2B clients in the United States — commerce built around quotes, services and qualified leads.
