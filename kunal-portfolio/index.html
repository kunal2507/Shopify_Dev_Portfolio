Engineering philosophy

A storefront is a machine for trust. Every millisecond, every layout shift, every extra tap is revenue leaking out of it.

Measured, not decorated

Work is judged in conversion lifts, faster load times and revenue — not shipped pages. If it can't be measured, it's a hypothesis, not a feature.

Real content first

Layouts and flows are shaped around real products, real copy and real customers — never lorem ipsum. The design follows the catalog, not the other way around.

Performance is a budget

Speed budgets are enforced from the first commit. A 95+ Lighthouse average holds across launches because it's a constraint, not an afterthought.
